WELCOME TO THE CAREER SECTION

Discover the various career paths you con follow after earning a degree in Aerospace engineering

aerospace eng

designs and develops aircraft spacecraft satellites and missiles. Works on aerodynamics propulsion and structural syatems. Normally employed by companies like NASA Boeing and Airbus to find and test the limits of flight.

Avionics engineering

specilizes in the electronic systems of an aircraft and spacecraft. works on navigation communication and flight control systems. ensures all onboard electronics function safely in extreme conditions.

propulsion engineering

Focuses on designing engines and propulsion systems for aircraft and rockets. Works to improve thrust, fuel efficiency and performance. Critical in developing next generation jet engines and rocket motors for space missions.

structural engineering

Analyses and designs the physical framework of aircraft and spacecraft. Ensures structures can withstand extreme stress, pressure and temperature. Uses materials like carbon fibre and titanium to build lightweight yet incredibly strong components..

test flight engineer

Works directly with pilots to test new aircraft and systems in real flight conditions. Collects and analyses data to verify performance and safety. A highly practical role bridging engineering and live operations.

systems-eng

Oversees the integration of all technical components within an aircraft or spacecraft. Ensures every subsystem works together seamlessly. Manages complex projects from design through to launch, coordinating across multiple engineering disciplines.​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​
