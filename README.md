# Mannyspace.com

Mannyspace.com is an educational and descovery website that acts as a navigative becon for future engineers and fresh out of school engineers to help them get the most out of their degrees

## problem statement
 Most aspiring aerospace engineers lack a centralised and accessible resource that guides them from initial interest through to career readiness. The absence of such a platform means talented individuals are lost to the industry not due to lack of ability, but lack of information and direction. Mannyspace exists to solve that.​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​
## solution
Mannyspace serves as a single accessible platform that brings together everything an aspiring aerospace engineer needs. From scholarships and institutions to career paths. Mannyspace removes the barriers of information and makes aerospace engineering feel achievable for everyone.

## objectives
Mannyspace.com aims to reach out and debunk common misconseptions about the aerospace engineering and the aerospace industry in general while simulaniously inspiring and naturing the next generation of aerospace engineers by providing a one stop station for questions they may have.
